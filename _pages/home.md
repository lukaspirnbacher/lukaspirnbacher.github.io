I am a PhD candidate in Economics at [WU Vienna University of Economics and Business](https://www.wu.ac.at/en/economics). I combine data, economic models and econometric techniques to learn about things we cannot observe directly: What are the effects of a merger that has not happened yet? How does price regulation affect consumer surplus? Do firms collude? More broadly, I am interested in *empirical industrial organization*, *regulatory economics* and *structural econometrics*.

You can find my [CV here](/files/cv_pirnbacher.pdf). Feel free to reach out to me by [email](mailto:lukas.pirnbacher@wu.ac.at).
