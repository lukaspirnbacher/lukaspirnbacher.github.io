I am a PhD candidate in the Department of Economics at [WU Vienna University of Economics and Business](https://www.wu.ac.at/en/economics). My research combines data, economic models and econometric techniques to learn about things we cannot observe directly: What are the expected price and non-price effects of a merger that has not happened yet? How does consumer surplus respond to a particular policy? Do firms collude? More broadly, I am interested in empirical industrial organization, regulatory economics and structural econometrics.

You can find my [CV here](/files/cv_pirnbacher.pdf). Feel free to reach out to me by [email](mailto:lukas.pirnbacher@wu.ac.at).
