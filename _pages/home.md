<h1 class="page__title">Welcome!</h1>

I am a PhD candidate at [WU Vienna University of Economics and Business](https://www.wu.ac.at/en/economics).

My research combines data, economic models and econometric techniques to learn about things we cannot observe directly: How would a proposed merger change market outcomes? How does a given policy affect welfare? Do firms collude? More broadly, I am interested in *empirical industrial organization*, *regulatory economics* and *structural econometrics*.

You can find my [CV here](/files/cv_pirnbacher.pdf). Feel free to reach out to me by [email](mailto:lukas.pirnbacher@wu.ac.at).
